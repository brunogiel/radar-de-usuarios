# Discovery Document

This is the living document of the process. It summarizes the state, connects the sub-documents and prevents the conversation from being the only memory of the project.

## How to use this document

- Keep it as an executive index, not a dump of everything.
- Link to or summarize the important parts of each sub-document.
- Separate facts from assumptions.
- Record gaps instead of hiding them.
- At the end of each gate, update the status and the next action.

- 1: I have an idea, but it's all assumptions.
- 2: I'm selling/testing something and learning by watching what works.
- 3: I know my users because I come from the field, but I haven't researched systematically.
- 4: I've interviewed users several times.
