# Source Distillate

Operational principles used to shape Radar. This file summarizes method ideas, not source documents.

## Author-Owned Facilitation Material

- Start by asking how well the team knows its users.
- Use a 0-5 maturity scale for user understanding.
- Treat the Design Challenge as the project anchor.
- Begin with what the team knows, what remains unknown and what it wants to learn.
- Separate user, customer, payer, decision-maker, operator and influencer.
- Use interview guides as flexible field aids.
- Treat the participant as the expert.
- Balance information gathering with trust-building.
- Ask about daily life, concrete experiences, reasons and ideal scenarios.
- Avoid leading, judging, interrupting or asking people to confirm a solution.
- Study context: where the behavior happens, what tools people use, what materials they share and what workarounds exist.
- Expect interviews to return as transcripts, notes and field materials. Process them person by person before comparing profiles.
- Move from data to insight to design principle. Every insight needs evidence.

## Human-Centered Design References

- Human-centered design applies to products, services, spaces and systems.
- Discovery alternates divergent and convergent work.
- Beginner's mind helps the team learn from participants with fewer prior assumptions.
- Diverse teams improve research quality.
- Research can combine people, experts, context and analogous inspiration.
- Include mainstream participants and useful extremes.
- Experts add context while user evidence remains the main source.
- Plan research with profiles, methods, agenda, permissions and roles.
- Respect privacy, consent, identity, power dynamics and local context.
- Capture exact quotes, observations, photos and materials with permission.
- Keep an evidence chain: raw material, person-level notes, atomic evidence, patterns and insights.
- Synthesis works best by sharing stories person by person, grouping themes, naming insights and writing HMW questions.
- HMW questions should stay centered on people, avoid embedded solutions and allow several possible answers.
